Adherence Tips & Tools

Small steps. Big impact.

Staying on track with your ARVs can be easier than you think. Here are some practical tips and tools to help you build the habit.

Build a Routine

Take your medication at the same time every day. Link it to a daily activity (e.g., brushing your teeth).

Use Reminders

Set alarms, use a pill box or medication app. Keep it visible (e.g., on your nightstand).

Manage Side Effects

Mild side effects can happen at first. If they persist or get worse, talk to your healthcare provider.

Get Support

Talk to a trusted friend, partner or support group. You're not alone.

Live a Healthy Lifestyle

Eat well, stay active, get enough rest, and manage stress.

Missed a Dose?

Take it as soon as you remember (unless it's almost time for the next one). Do not double dose.

Need more help?

Talk to your healthcare provider or contact the helpline for personalised advice.
