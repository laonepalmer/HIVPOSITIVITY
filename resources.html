Botswana support

Care and support close to home

Call ahead to confirm services, hours and what you need to bring. Your clinic can also help you arrange ARV care, testing or a referral.

⌂

University of Botswana Clinic

Block 120/127, University of Botswana campus, Gaborone. Offers consultation, HIV testing and counselling, HIV management, pharmacy and mental-health services for the UB community.

+267 355 4734Clinic reception

+267 72 800 421Medical emergency contact

Francistown primary clinics

Area W, Jubilee and Gerald clinics provide 24-hour outpatient services in Francistown. The council lists its health office for enquiries and clinic information.

+267 241 1050City of Francistown Council

Tebelopele clinics

Tebelopele provides HIV and STI screening, counselling and related sexual-health services across 18 locations, including Gaborone, Francistown, Maun and Palapye.

Government clinic near you

Government clinics can provide HIV testing, treatment support and referrals. If a service is not available at your nearest clinic, ask the nurse where to go next.
