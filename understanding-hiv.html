The basics

What is HIV?

HIV (human immunodeficiency virus) attacks the body’s immune system, especially CD4 cells. Without treatment, it can weaken the immune system over time.

What is AIDS?

AIDS (acquired immunodeficiency syndrome) is the most advanced stage of HIV infection. Effective HIV treatment can prevent HIV from progressing to AIDS.

✦

There is hope. With regular medical care and antiretroviral therapy (ARV), many people living with HIV can live long, healthy lives.

Clear it up

Myths vs facts

× Myth
You can get HIV from a hug, shared food or a toilet seat.
✓ Fact
HIV is not passed by casual contact, saliva, sharing food or hugs.
× Myth
HIV always means a short or unhealthy life.
✓ Fact
Treatment can control HIV and help people stay healthy.

Know the facts

How HIV is and isn’t passed on

HIV can be passed through

  • Blood
  • Semen and pre-seminal fluid
  • Vaginal and rectal fluids
  • Breast milk

HIV is not passed through

  • Hugging, kissing or sharing food
  • Toilet seats, swimming pools or insect bites
  • Everyday social contact
  • Working or going to school together

If you think you may have been exposed to HIV, contact a healthcare provider or clinic as soon as possible.
